Gifted Minds Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

Gifted Minds is an Android application that provides statistical analysis and number-selection tools for UK 49's Lunchtime and Teatime lottery draws.

1. Information We Collect

Gifted Minds is designed to provide its core functionality without requiring users to create an account.

Information entered into the application, such as lottery results and numbers used for analysis or number pairing, may be stored locally on the user's device to provide application features such as results and history.

Gifted Minds does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through its core lottery-analysis features.

2. How Information Is Used

Information stored within the application may be used to:

  • Display lottery results.
  • Maintain results history.
  • Perform statistical analysis.
  • Generate number-selection outputs.
  • Provide number-pairing functionality.
  • Improve the operation of the application.

3. Advertising

Gifted Minds may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services.

Third-party advertising providers may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Users should review the privacy policy of the relevant advertising provider for information about how advertising data is handled.

4. Third-Party Services

Gifted Minds may use third-party services to provide functionality such as advertising, application distribution, analytics, or other technical services.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies. Gifted Minds does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party service providers.

5. Data Storage

Core application data may be stored locally on the user's device. Removing the application or clearing its application data may remove locally stored information.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Gifted Minds is not specifically directed at children. The application should not be used by children where applicable law prohibits such use.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Users may uninstall Gifted Minds or clear its application data to remove locally stored application information.

Where applicable, users may also have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, objection, or restriction of personal information processed by third-party services.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Lottery Disclaimer

Gifted Minds provides statistical analysis and number-selection tools for informational and entertainment purposes.

Lottery results are random and no prediction, analysis, or number selection provided by the application guarantees a winning result. Users are responsible for complying with the laws and regulations applicable to them.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning Gifted Minds, please contact the application developer through the contact information provided on the official application listing.
